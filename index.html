Stronger Together. Stronger at Work

This platform was created by employees, for employees, to provide information about workplace organization and the power of collective action.

Our mission is to unite coworkers around the shared belief that every employee deserves fair treatment, transparent policies, consistent standards, and a meaningful voice in the decisions that affect our jobs and our families. Change begins when workers stand together. Learn your rights, stay informed, and help build a stronger future for everyone.

Why we should organize you ask?

Why Now?

In just a few short months, we've watched our workplace change in ways many of us never expected. Benefits we've grown accustomed to have disappeared, policies have changed without our input, and the stability we once relied on has been replaced with uncertainty. Today it's bonuses. Tomorrow it could be something else. The reality is simple: when employees have no collective voice, decisions that affect our jobs, our families, and our pay can be made without us. A union isn't about creating conflict—it's about making sure the people who keep this operation running finally have a seat at the table. Together, we have a voice. Individually, we only have opinions.

Fair Work Deserves Fair Treatment

Every one of us understands that performance matters. We take pride in working hard and earning our pay. But performance should be measured fairly—not by factors we can't control. When inventory is short, when forklift replenishment is delayed due to various reasons, or when operational issues slow production, those lost minutes don't belong to us. Yet we're expected to absorb those losses while still meeting the same production standards. At the same time, discipline has become increasingly unpredictable, with some employees facing immediate termination while others receive different outcomes for similar situations. Hard work should be rewarded—not overshadowed by inconsistent rules or circumstances beyond our control. Every employee deserves clear expectations, equal treatment, and a fair opportunity to be heard.

We Earned Our Pay—Why Make It Harder to Keep?

Without asking the people doing the work, the company raised the minimum performance needed to qualify for incentive pay from 95% to 103%, while leaving the rest of the incentive scale exactly the same. That means we're expected to work harder just to reach the same starting point we already earned before. The goalposts moved—but the reward didn't. Every percentage point matters when your paycheck depends on performance. If changes this significant can happen once, they can happen again. That's why having a collective voice matters. We deserve transparency, consistency, and a meaningful say in decisions that directly affect our income. Our work creates the success of this company—it should also earn us a voice in how we’re treated.
